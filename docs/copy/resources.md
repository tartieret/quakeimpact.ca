## Follow the province's preparedness guide

*PreparedBC, Province of British Columbia. A website and printable guides.*

The *Earthquake and Tsunami Preparedness Guide* asks households to be ready to look
after themselves for at least two weeks, and covers the plan, the supplies and what to
do while the ground is shaking. [PREPAREDBC] It is the guide the advice on
[Preparing](/prepare/) follows.

Beside it are a household plan to fill in, published in English, French, Chinese and
Punjabi, [PREPAREDBC-PLAN] [PREPAREDBC-GUIDES] and separate guides for apartments and
condominiums, for people with disabilities, for pets and for neighbourhoods.
[PREPAREDBC-GUIDES]

[Go to PreparedBC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/emergency-management/preparedbc) ·
[All the guides](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/emergency-management/preparedbc/guides-and-resources)

## Practise in the ShakeOut drill every October

*The Great British Columbia ShakeOut, run by the British Columbia Earthquake Alliance, a
not-for-profit society. An annual drill open to individuals, families, schools and
organizations.*

Once a year, at the same moment, people across the province stop and practise Drop,
Cover and Hold On. About 868,000 took part in 2025. [BCEA-SHAKEOUT-26] Sign up a
household, a classroom or a workplace, and rehearse the first minute with the people you
would be with. What to do while the shaking lasts is on [Preparing](/prepare/).

[Sign up at shakeoutbc.ca](https://www.shakeoutbc.ca/)

## Listen to Fault Lines, a CBC podcast

*CBC Vancouver, presented by Johanna Wagstaffe. Five episodes, 2016.*

The series takes a megathrust earthquake off the coast and a shallow one beneath
Vancouver, the same two kinds this site describes, and follows them through the first
day, the first three days, the first week, and the month and the year after. The last
episode hears from two people who lived through the earthquakes in Christchurch, New
Zealand, in 2010 and 2011. [CBC-FAULTLINES-16]

Where it and the province's current guide differ on what to keep at home, follow the
guide. Earthquake early warning, which can give seconds of notice before strong shaking
arrives, reached British Columbia in 2024, after the series was made. [NRCAN-EEW]

[CBC Listen](https://www.cbc.ca/listen/cbc-podcasts/147-fault-lines) ·
[Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/fault-lines/id1162124786)
